Files:

- README.txt: these instructions.

BEFORE PUBLISHING:
1. Open index.html in a text editor.
2. Find CLINIC_WHATSAPP_NUMBER = "91XXXXXXXXXX" and replace it with your clinic WhatsApp number using digits only, including 91. Do not include +, spaces, or dashes.
3. Add your real clinic Google Maps link and phone number if you want those displayed as direct links.
4. Confirm your qualifications, clinic details, services, and wording are accurate before publishing.
5. Add your own professional photo and clinic photos if desired; the current tooth illustration is a custom decorative SVG.

FREE HOSTING OPTION:
- You can publish this static site on a free static hosting service such as GitHub Pages or Cloudflare Pages, subject to their current terms and setup requirements.
- After publishing, connect drarkasaha.in by adding the DNS records supplied by that host in your BigRock DNS management panel.
- Do not change nameservers or DNS records until the host gives you the exact records.
- HTTPS/SSL may take some time to activate after DNS verification.

IMPORTANT:
- The enquiry form opens WhatsApp; it does not store submissions or automatically confirm appointments.
- Do not publish patient information, patient photographs, or testimonials without appropriate consent.
- This is a starter website. Review all clinical, professional and contact information before going live.
