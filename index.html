About the doctor

Meet Dr. Arka Saha

Dr. Arka Saha is a dental surgeon with clinical experience in general dentistry and a focused interest in periodontics and oral implantology. He completed his BDS at Haldia Institute of Dental Sciences and Research and has worked as a house surgeon at Balurghat District Hospital.

He has been practising in Krishnanagar since 2021 and is pursuing MDS training in Periodontics and Oral Implantology at K.S.D. Jain Dental College & Hospital, Kolkata.
