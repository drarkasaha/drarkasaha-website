Meet your dentist

A local connection.
A commitment to oral health.

Born and raised in Krishnanagar, Dr. Arka Saha is a dental surgeon with a focused professional interest in periodontal disease management and dental implant dentistry, alongside general dentistry and comprehensive oral healthcare.

He completed his BDS at Haldia Institute of Dental Sciences and Research (2015–2020), followed by service as a House Surgeon at Balurghat District Hospital, Dakshin Dinajpur (2020–2021). He is currently pursuing his MDS in Periodontology and Oral Implantology at Kusum Devi Sunderlal Dugar Jain Dental College and Hospital, Kolkata (2024–2027 batch).

Treatments & care

Care for gums, teeth, and your whole smile.

From gum health and implant assessment to everyday dental needs, treatment recommendations are based on clinical examination, diagnosis, and individual goals.

01

Gum Disease Management

Care focused on the health of the gums and supporting tissues of the teeth.

  • Gum assessment and periodontal diagnosis
  • Scaling and root surface debridement, when indicated
  • Periodontal maintenance and oral hygiene guidance
  • Assessment of gum recession and tooth mobility
02

Dental Implants

Individual assessment and planning for replacing missing teeth with implant-supported options.

  • Implant suitability assessment
  • Review of bone and gum conditions
  • Implant treatment planning and referral coordination where appropriate
  • Maintenance advice for implant health
03

General Dentistry

Essential dental care to help prevent, diagnose, and manage common oral health problems.

  • Routine dental examination
  • Preventive care and oral hygiene counselling
  • Assessment of tooth pain and dental concerns
  • Restorative care and treatment coordination
04

Comprehensive Oral Health

A broader view of oral health, considering teeth, gums, function, habits, and maintenance.

  • Personalised treatment planning
  • Prevention and risk-factor discussion
  • Follow-up and long-term maintenance
  • Guidance on when specialist care is needed
05

Tooth Replacement Planning

Discuss suitable options for missing teeth based on oral health, needs, and clinical findings.

  • Assessment of the site and surrounding tissues
  • Discussion of available treatment options
  • Planning that considers function and maintenance
06

Oral Health Advice

Practical guidance to help patients understand their oral health and make informed decisions.

  • Brushing and interdental cleaning guidance
  • Gum bleeding and bad breath assessment
  • Advice on regular reviews and maintenance

Know your oral health.

Clear information can help you recognise common concerns and know when to seek a dental assessment.

Gum health

Do your gums bleed?

Bleeding gums can be a sign of inflammation. A dental assessment can help identify the cause and appropriate care.

Missing teeth

Considering dental implants?
